## Product Description

While most vitamin E supplements contain only alpha-tocopherol, Life Extension delivers the full spectrum with an emphasis on gamma-tocopherol. Gamma-E has its own anti-inflammatory properties and is actually displaced by high-dose alpha-tocopherol — a key argument for mixed tocopherols.

## What We Like

- All four tocopherol forms, not just alpha

- Addresses the problem of alpha-tocopherol-only supplementation

## What We're Missing
