## Product Description

Life Extension pairs **20 mg FloraGLO lutein** with **1 mg zeaxanthin** from marigold extract. Capsules are small, contain minimal excipients, and integrate easily into an eye-and-screen support stack.

## What We Like

- **Branded FloraGLO ingredient:** consistent with most AREDS studies
- **Minimal-filler capsules:** no bulking matrix, easy to dose
- **cGMP manufacturing:** Life Extension documents every batch
- **Focused formula:** pure carotenoid profile rather than a mixed blend

## What We're Missing

- **No astaxanthin or vitamin E:** must be supplemented separately
- **Only 1 mg zeaxanthin:** those targeting AREDS values may need a maize or paprika extract combo
- **No vegan certification** despite plant-based ingredients

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule daily with a meal containing some fat. For screen-heavy days, up to 2 capsules may be considered after consulting a physician.
