## Product Description

NOW Foods offers psyllium husk in convenient capsule form. Psyllium is a soluble fiber that binds water and regulates digestion. EFSA has approved a health claim for cholesterol reduction.

## What We Like

- EFSA-approved health claim for cholesterol reduction
- 200 capsules — long-lasting supply
- Simple ingredient list with no additives
- Convenient for on-the-go (vs. powder)

## What We're Missing

- 3 capsules = 1.5 g — therapeutic doses (5–10 g) require powder
- Capsules must be taken with plenty of water (swelling risk)
- Loose powder is significantly cheaper per gram

## Usage Recommendation

3 capsules 1–3× daily with at least 250 ml water. Always stay well hydrated! For trouble swallowing: prefer powder.
