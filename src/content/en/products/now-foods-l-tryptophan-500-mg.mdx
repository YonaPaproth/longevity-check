## Product Description

NOW Foods L-Tryptophan delivers 500 mg of this essential amino acid, which serves as a precursor to both serotonin and melatonin. Unlike 5-HTP, L-tryptophan is also used for protein synthesis and niacin production.

## What We Like

- 500 mg per capsule — clinically relevant dose

- More versatile than 5-HTP (used in multiple metabolic pathways)

## What We're Missing

- 60 capsules = approximately 2 months
- Do not combine with SSRIs or MAO inhibitors

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule in the evening on an empty stomach, 30–60 minutes before sleep. Do not take with a protein-rich meal.
