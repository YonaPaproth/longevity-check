## Product Description

Each capsule combines all eight B vitamins in physiologically active forms: benfotiamine, riboflavin-5-phosphate, P5P, methylfolate, methylcobalamin, biotin, pantothenic acid, and niacinamide. Produced in Berlin with plant-based capsules.

## What We Like

- Active coenzyme forms — no cheap salts
- Made in Germany with QR code linking to lab values
- Free of magnesium stearate and silicates
- Includes inositol and choline as extras

## What We're Missing

- 50 mg niacinamide may cause flushing in sensitive individuals
- No separate AM/PM capsules

## Usage Recommendation

Take 1 capsule with a meal containing some fat. During high-stress periods, take 1 capsule morning and afternoon with plenty of water; avoid taking late in the evening.
