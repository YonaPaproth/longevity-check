## Product Description

Fairvital is a German direct-to-consumer supplement brand. This D3+K2 product delivers 1000 IU vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) combined with 200 µg vitamin K2 as MK-7 in a single daily capsule.

## What We Like

- **Price:** Barely any comparable product costs less than €0.10 per day
- **Made in Germany:** GMP-certified production under EU regulations
- **Sensible combo:** D3 and K2 MK-7 complement each other — K2 supports proper calcium utilization

## What We're Missing

- **Low D3 dose:** 1000 IU is insufficient for many deficient adults — typical recommendations are 2000–4000 IU
- **No third-party certification**
- **MK-7 source not disclosed:** Synthetic vs. fermented is not stated

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule daily with a fat-containing meal. Vitamin D is fat-soluble — absorption drops significantly without dietary fat.
