## Product Description

Ideal Bowel Support delivers 10 billion CFU of Lactobacillus plantarum 299v per capsule. The strain comes from Swedish biotech company Probi and is encapsulated in acid-resistant capsules.

## What We Like

- Clinical data specifically on post-antibiotic bloating
- Vegan DRcaps protect against stomach acid
- No FOS/inulin additives — suitable for low-FODMAP diets

## What We're Missing

- Single strain only — those seeking diversity need combination products
- Cold chain recommended, which complicates shipping

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule daily on an empty stomach or 30 minutes before a meal with water. When taking antibiotics, separate by at least 2 hours.
