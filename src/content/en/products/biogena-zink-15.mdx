## Product Description

BIOGENA uses zinc bisglycinate — a chelate compound that is particularly stomach-friendly and shows higher absorption rates than zinc oxide or sulfate. 15 mg elemental zinc per capsule equals 150% of the NRV.

## What We Like

- Bisglycinate form for high bioavailability
- Very good stomach tolerance
- Only one capsule daily
- No unnecessary fillers

## What We're Missing

- 15 mg is moderate — possibly insufficient for diagnosed deficiency
- Significantly more expensive than discount zinc
- No copper as antagonist included

## Recommended Dosage

1 capsule daily, ideally on an empty stomach or with a light meal. Do not take simultaneously with iron, calcium, or coffee.
