## Product Description

BIOGENA Turmeric 500 Plus combines turmeric extract standardized to 95% curcuminoids with piperine (from black pepper), which can increase the bioavailability of curcumin by up to 20 times.

## What We Like

- 95% curcuminoid standardization
- Piperine as proven absorption enhancer
- Clean capsules without unnecessary additives
- Austrian pharmaceutical grade

## What We're Missing

- Piperine can cause issues for sensitive stomachs
- No modern bioavailability technology (micelles, phytosomes)
- Priced above market average

## Recommended Dosage

1 capsule daily with a meal. Do not take on an empty stomach as piperine can irritate the stomach.
