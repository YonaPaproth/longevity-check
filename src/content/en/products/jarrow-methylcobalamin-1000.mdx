## Product Description

1000 mcg methylcobalamin in a sublingual lozenge that dissolves quickly, supporting absorption through the oral mucosa. Ideal for those with low animal product intake or who want to lower homocysteine.

- **Active form:** Methylcobalamin bypasses potential conversion issues with cyanocobalamin

- **Value:** Large pack lasts over three months

Place 1 tablet under the tongue daily and let it dissolve fully. Best taken in the morning on an empty stomach or between meals so the mucosa is clear.
