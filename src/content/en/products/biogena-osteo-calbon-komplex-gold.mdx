## Product Description

BIOGENA Osteo Calbon Komplex Gold combines six bone-relevant actives in one product. The centerpiece is Calbon-N — microcrystalline hydroxyapatite from natural sources, delivering calcium and phosphorus in the natural ratio (as found in bone itself). Complemented by coral calcium, vitamin D3, K2 as K2VITAL (all-trans MK-7), silicon from bamboo shoot extract, and boron.

## What We Like

- Hydroxyapatite instead of isolated calcium carbonate — delivers calcium + phosphorus in the natural ratio
- K2VITAL (all-trans MK-7) — the most effective K2 form
- 750 mg calcium per daily dose — clinically relevant amount
- Silicon from bamboo (52 mg) + boron (3 mg) — often overlooked bone cofactors
- Biogena pharmaceutical quality from Austria
- Smart combination: D3 for calcium absorption, K2 to direct calcium into bones instead of arteries

## What We're Missing

- 3 capsules per day — a lot to swallow
- €1.33/day is the highest price in our bone segment
- 90 capsules = only 1 month supply
- No magnesium included (a commonly recommended bone cofactor)
- Hydroxyapatite has fewer absorption studies than calcium citrate

## Usage Recommendation

3 capsules daily with meals, ideally split (e.g., 2 in the morning, 1 in the evening). Calcium absorption is limited at single doses above 500 mg.
