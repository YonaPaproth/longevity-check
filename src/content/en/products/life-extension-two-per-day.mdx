## Product Description

Two-Per-Day combines active B-vitamins (methylfolate, P5P, methylcobalamin) with carotenoids, minerals, and trace elements. Two vegetarian capsules serve as a solid micronutrient foundation.

## What We Like

- Active forms of B6, B12, and folate
- Contains genuine RRR-alpha-tocopherol plus tocotrienols
- Full-spectrum carotenoid blend
- Good value with subscription or bulk purchase

## What We're Missing

- No vitamin K2 included
- Iron-free — some women may need a separate iron supplement

## Usage Recommendation

2 capsules daily with a fat-containing meal. If stomach-sensitive, split into 1 capsule morning and 1 evening.
