## Product Description

The D,L-form combines both isomers: L-phenylalanine is converted to L-tyrosine → dopamine → noradrenaline. D-phenylalanine is thought to inhibit enkephalinase, prolonging endogenous endorphins (natural pain modulation).

## What We're Missing
