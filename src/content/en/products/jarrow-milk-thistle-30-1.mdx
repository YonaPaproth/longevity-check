## Product Description

Jarrow extracts milk thistle seeds with ethanol, concentrates them to a 30:1 ratio, and encapsulates the powder in plant-based capsules. Each serving delivers 150 mg silymarin complex.

## What We Like

- High extraction ratio reduces pill count
- Tested for heavy metals and aflatoxins
- Vegan and low-allergen
- Pairs well with NAC or TMG

## What We're Missing

- No phospholipid matrix like Siliphos for enhanced absorption
- Only 60 servings per bottle

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule twice daily with meals, especially fatty or alcohol-containing ones. For long-term use, take 1–2 week breaks periodically.
