## Product Description

Synomed Multivit + Mineral + Spurenelemente is a classic German pharmacy product combining all important B-vitamins, minerals, and trace elements in one tablet. 2 tablets daily from a pack of 100 tablets for 50 days' supply.

## What We Like

- B-vitamins at 200% NRV — solid coverage for active individuals
- Gluten- and lactose-free
- Pharmacy-grade quality control
- Convenient 50-day supply per pack
- Affordable for a complete formula

## What We're Missing

- Zinc only 1.5 mg — daily requirement is 10–16 mg, barely more than symbolic
- Minerals at ~15% NRV: insufficient for those with genuine deficiency
- Magnesium at 60 mg not adequate for supplementation purposes
- No bioactive B-vitamin forms (no methylcobalamin, no P5P)
- Iron at 2.1 mg insufficient for women of childbearing age

## Usage Recommendation

2 tablets daily with a meal. Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) are absorbed better with dietary fat.
