## Product Description

NOW delivers copper as **copper gluconate**, a well-tolerated salt form. Each tablet provides 2 mg, making it simple to add alongside high zinc doses.

## What We Like

- **Precise 2 mg dose** rather than oversized 5–10 mg tablets
- **Non-GMO and GMP**-manufactured
- **Tablets easy to split** for a 1 mg dose
- **Very affordable price**

## What We're Missing

- **Contains cellulose and stearates** — may not appeal to purists
- **Not a chelated form**, though gluconate absorbs well
- **No zinc combination product** — requires two separate supplements

## Usage Recommendation

1 tablet every 1–2 days with a meal. If taking ≥25 mg zinc daily, take 1 tablet daily. Do not exceed 2 mg without blood work guidance.
