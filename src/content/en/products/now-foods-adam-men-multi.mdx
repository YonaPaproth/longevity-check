## Product Description

ADAM Men's Multi is formulated to address typical men's needs such as prostate support, heart health, and energy. It contains vitamins, minerals, borage oil, saw palmetto, lycopene, and coenzymated B-vitamins.

## What We Like

- Includes herbs like saw palmetto and nettle root
- Provides lycopene and alpha-lipoic acid
- Good value at 90 servings
- Capsules rather than tablets — easier to swallow

## What We're Missing

- No NSF/USP seal
- Contains traces of soy and wheat from some extracts

## Usage Recommendation

2 capsules daily with a meal. If caffeine-sensitive, avoid taking alongside morning coffee, as B-vitamins can have a stimulating effect.
