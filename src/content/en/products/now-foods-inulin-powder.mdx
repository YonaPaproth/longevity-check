## Product Description

NOW Foods Inulin Prebiotic Pure Powder contains **5 g of pure inulin per serving** (227 g, approximately 45 servings). Inulin is a natural prebiotic fiber from chicory root that serves as a food source for beneficial gut bacteria (especially Bifidobacteria). Powder form enables flexible dosing — from 2.5 g to 10 g daily.
