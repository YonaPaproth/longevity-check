## Product Description

Sunfood encapsulates 1000 mg vitamin C in liposomal phospholipids from sunflower lecithin. The liquid comes in convenient sachets with a mild orange flavor.

## What We Like

- **Liposomal technology** for higher plasma levels
- **Sunflower lecithin instead of soy**
- **Sachet portions**: ideal for travel
- **No added sugar**

## What We're Missing

- **Premium price** compared to powder
- **Only 30 servings — one-month supply**
- **Slightly bitter taste** — usually needs to be mixed in juice

## Usage Recommendation

1 sachet daily, taken straight or mixed with 100 ml water, ideally before a meal. Knead the sachet before use; chilled, the taste is more neutral.
