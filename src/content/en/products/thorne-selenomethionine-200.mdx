## Product Description

Thorne has long relied on hypoallergenic formulations. This capsule combines 200 mcg selenomethionine with microcrystalline cellulose as a carrier, with no magnesium stearate or colorants.

## What We Like

- NSF Sport certification appeals to competitive athletes
- Organic form is slowly released and storable in the body
- Gluten-, soy-, and dairy-free
- US manufacturer with decades of reputation

## What We're Missing

- No combination with vitamin E or C for synergistic effects
- Fixed 200 mcg dose, capsule cannot be split

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule daily with a meal. If on thyroid medication, separate timing and consult a doctor.
