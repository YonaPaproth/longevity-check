## Review

iXora's KSM-66 Ashwagandha delivers 600 mg of standardised root extract per day across two capsules. The dose aligns with the most-cited RCTs. The product avoids proprietary blends and inflated serving sizes, which earns marks for label honesty.

### What we like

- Uses authentic KSM-66 (standardised to ≥5% withanolides) — the extract used in the majority of stress and sleep RCTs
- 600 mg/day matches the clinically studied range
- Clean capsule: no artificial colours, unnecessary additives, or proprietary blends
- Transparent labelling with milligram amounts clearly stated

### What could be better

- No independent third-party certification (Informed Sport, NSF, USP)
- CoA available on request only, not proactively published

### Verdict

For consumers looking for a no-frills ashwagandha supplement at the studied dose, this product delivers. We recommend it with the caveat that third-party testing transparency could be improved.
