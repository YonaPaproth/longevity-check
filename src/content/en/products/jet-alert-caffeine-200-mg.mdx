## Product Description

Jet-Alert delivers 200 mg pure caffeine per tablet — equivalent to about 2 cups of coffee. Minimalist, functional, and extremely affordable.

## What We Like

- €0.06/day — the cheapest supplement in our catalog
- 200 mg per tablet — precise dosing vs. variable coffee content
- 120 tablets = 4 months' supply

## What We're Missing

- No L-theanine included (which reduces caffeine side effects)
- Can cause heart palpitations, anxiety, and sleep disruption in sensitive individuals
- No slow-release formulation

## Usage Recommendation

1 tablet in the morning or before exercise. Do not take after 2 pm. Max 400 mg/day (EFSA). Best combined with 100–200 mg L-theanine.
