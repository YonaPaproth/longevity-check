## Product Description

Alive By Science offers NMN as a lozenge that dissolves under the tongue, largely bypassing the digestive tract for faster absorption into the bloodstream. Each tablet contains 125 mg of high-purity Beta-NMN.

## What We Like

- Sublingual delivery for rapid absorption without water
- Blister packaging protects against moisture and light
- HPLC certificates available for every batch
- Compact travel format, no powder measuring needed

## What We're Missing

- Contains stevia, which not everyone likes
- Tablets are sensitive to heat — do not store in a hot car

## Usage Recommendation

Place 1 tablet under the tongue and let it fully dissolve, ideally in the morning. For higher doses, use up to 2 tablets spread throughout the day.
