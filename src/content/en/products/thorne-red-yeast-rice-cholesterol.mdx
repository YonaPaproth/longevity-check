## Product Description

Thorne combines red yeast rice with coenzyme Q10. Red yeast rice contains monacolin K — chemically identical to the statin lovastatin. Thorne adds CoQ10 because statins (and monacolin K) can suppress the body's own CoQ10 production.

## What We Like

- Smart combination: monacolin K + CoQ10 to counter statin-typical CoQ10 depletion
- Thorne quality with NSF certification
- Standardized monacolin K content (not guaranteed by all suppliers)
- 120 capsules = 2 months at 2 capsules/day

## What We're Missing

- EFSA restricted monacolin K health claims in 2022 (>3 mg/day no longer permitted)
- Monacolin K is a natural statin — same side effects possible (myopathy, liver values)
- Do not take without medical supervision, especially if already on statin therapy
- Citrinin contamination is a risk with low-quality red yeast rice (Thorne controls for this)

## Usage Recommendation

2 capsules daily with a meal. Monitor liver values and lipid profile regularly. Do not combine with prescription statins.
