## Product Description

NatureWise uses natural d-alpha-tocopherol from sunflower oil — more bioavailable than synthetic dl-alpha-tocopherol. 400 IU (268 mg) per softgel, soy-free.

## What We Like

- Natural d-alpha-tocopherol (roughly twice the bioavailability of synthetic)
- From sunflower oil, soy-free
- 120 softgels = 4 months' supply
- Very affordable price

## What We're Missing

- Alpha-tocopherol only — no mixed tocopherol spectrum (gamma, delta)
- The SELECT trial showed increased prostate cancer risk with long-term high-dose use
- 400 IU slightly exceeds the EFSA upper limit of 300 mg/day

## Usage Recommendation

1 softgel daily with a fatty meal. Do not take high doses long-term without medical guidance. Consult a doctor if you take anticoagulants or have a bleeding disorder.
