## Product Description

Synomed Zink Tabletten delivers 10 mg elemental zinc daily as zinc gluconate (2 tablets of 5 mg each). Zinc is an essential trace element for the immune system, hormone balance, and cellular protection.

## What We Like

- 10 mg zinc daily — adequate preventive dose
- Pharmacy-grade quality and control
- Affordable compared to premium brands
- Simple dosing with 2 small tablets

## What We're Missing

- Zinc gluconate has significantly lower bioavailability than bisglycinate or picolinate
- For equivalent absorption, chelated zinc would be more effective
- No information on cofactors (copper balance important for long-term use)
- No guidance on optimal timing (taking on an empty stomach maximizes absorption)

## Usage Recommendation

2 tablets daily, preferably on an empty stomach or between meals for better absorption. Take with a meal if stomach upset occurs. Long-term use above 25 mg daily should be balanced with copper supplementation.
