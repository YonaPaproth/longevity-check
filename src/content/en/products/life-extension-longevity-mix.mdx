## Product Description

Life Extension Mix Tablets provide a broad foundation of active B vitamins, organic minerals, carotenoids, standardized extracts from broccoli, green tea, and resveratrol, plus trace minerals like boron.

## What We Like

- Active coenzyme forms (P-5-P, methylfolate, methylcobalamin)
- Plant-based antioxidants including quercetin, PQQ, and lutein
- Tablets can be split if a half dose is sufficient

## What We're Missing

- Some minerals like magnesium are only token-dosed
- Tablets are relatively large and can be difficult to swallow

## Usage Recommendation

Split 3 tablets across meals containing fat and protein to improve absorption of fat-soluble carotenoids.
