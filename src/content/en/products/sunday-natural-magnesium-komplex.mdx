## Product Description

The Magnesium Complex combines bisglycinate, malate, citrate, taurate, orotate, gluconate, and lactate. Each daily serving delivers 300 mg elemental magnesium, complemented by BioPerine for improved absorption.

## What We Like

- Multiple forms cover different gut and tissue preferences
- Produced and filled in Berlin
- Free of magnesium stearate and colorants
- Glass packaging instead of plastic

## What We're Missing

- Contains black pepper extract, which some people don't tolerate
- Only 90 servings; no refill packs available

## Usage Recommendation

Take 2 capsules daily, ideally split between morning and evening. Take with meals if you have a sensitive stomach.
