## Product Description

Sunday Natural delivers a highly concentrated omega-3 fish oil in natural triglyceride form, which according to studies is absorbed approximately 70% better than ethyl esters. Molecular distillation removes heavy metals and oxidation products.

## What We Like

- Triglyceride form for optimal bioavailability
- IFOS 5-star certification for purity and freshness
- 120 softgels = 4 months at 1 capsule/day
- Very attractive price for this quality level

## What We're Missing

- No visible MSC or Friend-of-the-Sea seal
- Fish oil — no vegan option (algae oil available separately in product range)
- Softgels relatively large

## Dosage Recommendation

1 softgel daily with a fatty meal. For increased needs (e.g., high triglycerides) up to 2 softgels after medical consultation.
