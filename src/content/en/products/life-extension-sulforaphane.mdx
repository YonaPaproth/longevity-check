## Product Description

Each capsule contains 400 mg BroccoRaphanin concentrate plus a separate myrosinase enzyme from radish seeds, so sulforaphane is produced during digestion rather than being pre-formed.

## What We Like

- Glucoraphanin and active enzyme in one capsule
- Vegan capsules, no dyes
- Delivers a standardized amount — more reliable than broccoli sprouts

## What We're Missing

- Only 30 servings — costs add up quickly
- May cause sulfur odor in those with sensitive digestion

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule daily with a light meal; no need to chew or open the capsule. Eating a small amount of mustard or radish alongside can improve sulforaphane conversion.
