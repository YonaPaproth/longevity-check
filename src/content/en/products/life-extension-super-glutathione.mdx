## Product Description

Each capsule contains 100 mg reduced glutathione (Setria), 500 mg L-cysteine, and 500 mg vitamin C. The combination targets a stable redox balance and supports endogenous glutathione synthesis.

## What We're Missing

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule with a morning meal. Under increased oxidative load, add a second capsule in the afternoon and drink plenty of water.
