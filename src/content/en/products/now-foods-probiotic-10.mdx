## Product Description

Probiotic-10 combines ten strains including L. rhamnosus, L. plantarum, B. lactis, and Streptococcus thermophilus. Vegetarian capsules are gluten-free and contain fructooligosaccharides as a prebiotic carrier.

## What We Like
