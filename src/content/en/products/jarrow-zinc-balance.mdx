## Product Description

Zinc Balance combines TRAACS zinc L-monomethionine (15 mg) with 1 mg copper gluconate. Capsules are made from vegetable cellulose and are gluten-free.

## What We Like

- Balanced ratio reduces copper depletion risk
- Chelated form is gentle on the stomach
- Best-value option in the trace mineral category

## What We're Missing

- No amino acids or vitamin C for enhanced uptake
- 1 mg copper may still be insufficient at high zinc doses

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule daily with a meal. For higher zinc needs (e.g. 30 mg), split doses or add a separate zinc source.
