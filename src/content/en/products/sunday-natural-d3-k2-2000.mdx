## Product Description

Sunday Natural combines vegan vitamin D3 from lichens with vitamin K2 in all-trans MK-7 form. The combination makes sense because K2 directs the calcium mobilized by D3 specifically into the bones rather than into the blood vessels.

## What We Like

- Vegan D3 from lichens — also suitable for vegetarians and vegans
- All-trans MK-7 — the most bioactive and stable K2 form
- 120 capsules = 4 months duration
- Excellent value for money

## What We're Missing

- 2000 I.U. may be too low in winter for some people
- No oil-based format (fat-soluble vitamins benefit from this)
- No independent third-party seal (NSF, USP)

## Dosage Recommendation

1 capsule daily with a fatty meal. In winter, possibly 2 capsules after medical consultation.
