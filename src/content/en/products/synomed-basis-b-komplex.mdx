## Product Description

The Synomed Basis-B-Komplex contains all 8 essential B-vitamins in their bioactive forms, supplemented with choline, betaine, and inositol as important cofactors. Key feature: B12 as methylcobalamin, B6 as pyridoxal-5-phosphate (P5P), and B9 as L-methylfolate — the directly usable forms.

## What We Like

- Methylcobalamin (B12): directly usable, no conversion by liver required
- P5P (B6): active form that works even with impaired liver function
- L-methylfolate (B9): essential for MTHFR carriers, standard folic acid isn't converted
- Choline, betaine, inositol as sensible additions
- All 8 B-vitamins fully covered
- 60 capsules for 2 months at a fair price

## What We're Missing

- No publicly available third-party testing certificates
- Individual B-vitamin dosages not reviewed in detail (manufacturer should make these more transparent)
- For very high B12 needs, separate individual dosing may be more appropriate

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule daily with a meal. B-vitamins are water-soluble — consistent daily intake is more important than the exact time of day.
