## Product Description

BIOGENA Omega 3 Premium delivers highly concentrated fish oil in the more bioavailable triglyceride form. The capsules are molecularly distilled to minimize heavy metals and contaminants.

## What We Like

- Triglyceride form instead of ethyl ester — approximately 70% better absorption according to studies
- IFOS 5-star rating for purity and freshness
- High EPA+DHA content per capsule
- Small capsules, minimal aftertaste

## What We're Missing

- Priced above market average
- No visible Friend-of-the-Sea or MSC seal
- Fish oil — not suitable for vegans (algae oil alternative available)

## Recommended Dosage

1 capsule daily with a fat-containing meal for optimal absorption.
