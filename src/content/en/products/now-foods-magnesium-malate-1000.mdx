## Product Description

NOW Foods combines magnesium with malic acid, delivering 1000 mg magnesium malate (approximately 125 mg elemental magnesium) per 3-tablet serving. Popular among those dealing with muscle tension or fibromyalgia.

## What We Like

- Very high serving count per bottle
- Malate supports energy metabolism

## What We're Missing

- Three tablets per serving is bulky

## Usage Recommendation

3 tablets daily, spread throughout the day with meals. Drink extra water if you have a sensitive stomach.
