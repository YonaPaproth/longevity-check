## Product Description

Life Extension is one of the oldest US supplement brands, founded in 1980. Optimized Resveratrol combines 100 mg trans-resveratrol with quercetin and fisetin — two related polyphenols with similar proposed mechanisms.

## What We Like

- **Trans-form:** Only the trans-configuration is biologically active — correctly declared
