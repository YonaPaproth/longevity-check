## Product Description

Each lozenge delivers **18.75 mg zinc as zinc acetate**. The format is particularly suited for the first signs of a cold, as the active compound is released directly via the oral mucosa.

## What We Like

- **Zinc acetate over gluconate:** better evidence base for shortening cold duration
- **Pleasant lozenge taste** without excessive sweeteners
- **cGMP manufacturer** with a reliable supply chain
- **Low cost per serving**

## What We're Missing

- **Sugar alcohols:** may cause GI discomfort in sensitive individuals
- **No copper supplementation** for long-term use
- **Contains citric acid**, which may be relevant for sensitive teeth

## Usage Recommendation

At the first sign of a sore throat, let 1 lozenge dissolve every 2–3 hours, up to 6 per day, for no longer than one week. For maintenance use, choose lower daily doses instead.
