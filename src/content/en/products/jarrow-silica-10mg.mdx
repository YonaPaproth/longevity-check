## Product Description

JarroSil delivers **choline-stabilized orthosilicic acid (ch-OSA)**, better known as BioSil. The liquid is taken with a small dosing cap and provides 10 mg silicon per 10 drops.

## What We Like

- **Original BioSil ingredient**
- **High serving count:** 100 servings per bottle
- **Amber glass** protects against light degradation
- **Mixes into water or juice** easily

## What We're Missing

- **Slightly mineral-bitter taste**
- **Daily drop-counting** can be inconvenient
- **No combination with collagen or vitamin C**

## Usage Recommendation

Stir 10 drops into water or juice and drink once daily before a meal. Shake the bottle before use and store cool after opening, but not in the refrigerator.
