## Product Description

BIOGENA CoQ10 100 Active delivers Coenzyme Q10 in a liposomal formulation that, according to the manufacturer, achieves up to three times higher bioavailability compared to conventional ubiquinone. 100 mg per capsule.

## What We Like

- Liposomal formulation for improved absorption
- 100 mg CoQ10 per capsule — clinically relevant dose
- Clean formulation without magnesium stearate
- Pharmaceutical grade from Austria

## What We're Missing

- Ubiquinone instead of ubiquinol — the latter is the reduced, directly usable form
- Significantly more expensive than conventional CoQ10 products
- 60 capsules = 2 months, relatively small package

## Recommended Dosage

1 capsule daily with a fat-containing meal. CoQ10 is fat-soluble and is absorbed better with fat.
