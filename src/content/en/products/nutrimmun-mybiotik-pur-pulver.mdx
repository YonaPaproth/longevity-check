## Product Description

**Nutrimmun MyBIOTIK Pur Powder** is an 11-strain probiotic supplement in single-serve sachets (30 × 2 g). It contains 2 billion colony-forming units (CFU) from eleven bacterial strains that are natural components of the human gut flora.

All strains are fully declared with W-codes, enabling traceability.

## What We Like

- Full strain declaration with W-code traceability system
- Vegan, gluten-free, lactose-free, no colorings or sweeteners
- Pharmacy-grade quality control
- Made in Germany
- 11 compatible strains covering both small and large intestine

## What We'd Improve

- Only 2 billion CFU — competitors like Physician's Choice offer 60 billion
- No public third-party certification (e.g. NSF, Informed Sport)
- No prebiotic included (separate Biofibre product required)

## How to Take It

Dissolve 1 sachet daily in water or a cold beverage, preferably in the morning before breakfast. Do not mix with hot liquids (destroys the bacterial cultures).
