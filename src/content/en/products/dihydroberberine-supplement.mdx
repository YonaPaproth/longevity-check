## Product Description

Dihydroberberine (DHB) is the active metabolite that berberine is converted into in the gut. GlucoVantage bypasses this step by delivering DHB directly — with up to 5× higher bioavailability at a lower dose. 200 mg DHB is considered equivalent to 1000–1500 mg standard berberine.

## What We Like

- 5× higher bioavailability than standard berberine
- Lower dose needed (200 mg instead of 1000+ mg)
- Significantly fewer gastrointestinal side effects
- GlucoVantage is a patented, clinically validated ingredient

## What We're Missing

- Less direct study evidence than standard berberine (most RCTs use berberine HCl)
- Higher daily cost than standard berberine
- Multiple brands use GlucoVantage — end-product quality varies
- Long-term safety data specific to DHB is still limited

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule (200 mg) 1–2× daily with a meal. Do not combine with metformin without medical advice (both lower blood sugar).
