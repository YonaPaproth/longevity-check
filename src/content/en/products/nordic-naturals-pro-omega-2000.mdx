## Product Description

Pro Omega 2000 delivers concentrated fish oil in natural triglyceride form. Two softgels provide approximately 1250 mg EPA and 750 mg DHA, with a lemon flavor to reduce aftertaste.

## What We Like

- IFOS 5-star certification
- Very high EPA+DHA density per softgel
- Traceable wild-caught fish source
- Triglyceride form improves bioavailability

## What We're Missing

- Noticeably more expensive than standard fish oils
- Large softgels — not easy to swallow for everyone

## Usage Recommendation

2 softgels daily with a fat-rich meal. For a sensitive stomach, freeze the softgels and take immediately before eating.
