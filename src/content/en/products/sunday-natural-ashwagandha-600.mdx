Sunday Natural uses the patented KSM-66 ashwagandha root extract — the world's most studied ashwagandha extract with over 20 clinical studies. Standardized to at least 5% withanolides via milk extraction (no chemical solvents).

## What We Like

- KSM-66 — the gold standard among ashwagandha extracts
- 600 mg per capsule corresponds to the most frequently used dose in studies
- 90 capsules = 3 months duration
- Vegan cellulose capsules without additives

## What We're Missing

- EFSA has not approved health claims for ashwagandha
- Can affect thyroid hormones — caution with Hashimoto's
- Not suitable for pregnant women

## Dosage Recommendation

1 capsule daily, ideally in the evening. For stress focus: in the morning. Take for at least 8 weeks for full effect.
