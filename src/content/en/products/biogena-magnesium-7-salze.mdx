## Product Description

BIOGENA uses seven different compounds for this magnesium — including glycinate, citrate, malate, and taurate. The idea: different magnesium salts are absorbed through different transport mechanisms, which should optimize overall absorption.

## What We Like

- Seven magnesium forms for broader absorption
- 300 mg elemental magnesium per daily dose
- Vegan capsule shell, no unnecessary fillers
- Austrian pharmaceutical grade with complete analytics

## What We're Missing

- Significantly more expensive than single-salt products (e.g., pure glycinate)
- No independent third-party seal (NSF, USP)
- Clinical evidence for multi-salt advantage over single forms is limited

## Recommended Dosage

2 capsules daily with a meal. For sensitive stomachs, divide into two doses (morning/evening).
