## Product Description

The combination of vitamin D3 with K2 (MK-7) makes sense because K2 directs calcium mobilized by D3 into bones instead of blood vessels. BIOGENA uses the most stable all-trans form of MK-7.

## What We Like

- All-trans MK-7 — the most bioactive and stable K2 form
- Vegan D3 from lichen possible
- 120 capsules = 4 months supply
- Minimal excipients

## What We're Missing

- 2000 I.U. is too low for some (especially in winter)
- No oil-based format (fat-soluble vitamins benefit from this)
- Priced above discount alternatives

## Recommended Dosage

1 capsule daily with a fat-containing meal. In winter, possibly 2 capsules after consultation with your doctor.
