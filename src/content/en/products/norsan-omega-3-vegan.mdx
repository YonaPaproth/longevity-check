## Product Description

Omega-3 Vegan combines Schizochytrium sp. algae oil with cold-pressed organic olive oil and a light lemon flavor. Each 5 ml serving delivers approximately 2000 mg DHA+EPA with no fish ingredients.

## What We Like

- High DHA+EPA dose per serving
- Fully vegan with sustainably fermented algae
- NORSAN publishes TOTOX values for each batch
- Glass bottle with measuring spoon included

## What We're Missing

- Requires refrigeration; only 2 months shelf life after opening
- Slightly algae-like taste — not for everyone

## Usage Recommendation

Take 5 ml daily with the included spoon, preferably with a fat-containing meal. Refrigerate after opening and shake before use.
