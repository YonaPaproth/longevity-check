Sports Research combines Curcumin C3 Complex — one of the most studied curcumin ingredients worldwide — with BioPerine (piperine extract) in a coconut oil softgel. C3 Complex is standardized to 95% curcuminoids and used in over 60 clinical studies.

## What We Like

- C3 Complex — one of the most frequently used curcumin ingredients in clinical trials

1 softgel daily with a meal. Do not take on an empty stomach (piperine). For joint issues, up to 2 softgels after consultation.
