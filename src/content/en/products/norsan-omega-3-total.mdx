## Product Description

NORSAN is a Berlin-based German company specializing in high-quality omega-3 oils. Omega-3 Total is a liquid fish oil with natural orange flavor, delivering approximately 1600–2000 mg EPA+DHA per teaspoon (5 ml).

## What We Like

- **IFOS certification:** International Fish Oil Standards — the strictest independent quality standard for fish oil
- **High dose:** More EPA+DHA per euro than most capsule products
- **Taste:** The orange flavor makes daily intake considerably more pleasant than many alternatives
- **Made in Germany:** Shorter supply chain, easier traceability
- **TÜV-tested:** Additional contaminant testing

## What We're Missing

- **Liquid form:** Requires refrigeration after opening; less practical for travel
- **No capsule option at the same dose:** Those preferring capsules need to look elsewhere

## Usage Recommendation

1 teaspoon daily with a fat-containing meal. Do not heat. Once opened, refrigerate and use within 3 months.
