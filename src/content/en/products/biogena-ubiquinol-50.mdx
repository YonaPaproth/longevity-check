## Product Description

Ubiquinol is the reduced, bioactive form of Coenzyme Q10. The body must first convert conventional ubiquinone — this step becomes less efficient with age. BIOGENA uses Kaneka ubiquinol, the world's leading branded raw material.

## What We Like

- Active ubiquinol form — directly bioavailable
- Kaneka raw material (Japanese market leader)
- Softgel protects oxidation-sensitive ubiquinol
- Pharmaceutical grade from Austria

## What We're Missing

- Only 50 mg per softgel — clinical studies typically use 100–200 mg
- High price per mg compared to market
- Requires 2–4 capsules for therapeutic dosing, which doubles costs

## Usage Recommendation

1 softgel daily with a fat-containing meal. With statin use, possibly 2 softgels after consulting your doctor.
