## Product Description

The bottle contains 90 vegetable capsules; each delivers 150 mg elemental magnesium from magnesium citrate. Free from gluten, dairy, soy, and artificial dyes.

## What We Like

- Well tolerated even at 300 mg
- Free from stearates and titanium dioxide
- 90 servings — a three-month supply
- Transparent label including total citrate weight

## What We're Missing

- No blended magnesium option (glycinate, malate)
- Citrate can have a mild laxative effect in sensitive individuals

## Usage Recommendation

1 capsule with a meal; for higher needs, take 1 capsule in the morning and 1 in the evening. Take with plenty of water and spread doses throughout the day.
