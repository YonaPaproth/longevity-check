## Mechanism of Action

Pumpkin seed oil from *Cucurbita pepo* contains phytosterols (particularly beta-sitosterol and delta-7 sterols), unsaturated fatty acids, and carotenoids. The proposed mechanisms in BPH include mild inhibition of 5-alpha-reductase (similar to finasteride but significantly weaker) and anti-inflammatory effects via phytosterols.

## Evidence

The largest study (Vahlensieck 2015, n=1431) showed significant IPSS improvements in mild BPH. However, the effect size was moderate and the study was partially industry-funded. For bladder health in women, initial positive data exists (Nishimura 2014) but with a small sample size only.

## Safety

Pumpkin seed oil is considered very safe. No notable side effects reported in studies. May cause mild gastrointestinal discomfort in rare cases.

## Dosage

1000–3000 mg pumpkin seed oil per day, typically divided into 2-3 doses with meals.
